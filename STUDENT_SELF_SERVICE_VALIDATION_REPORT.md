## 3. Conflicting or Unclear Instructions in the Manual

The following internal contradictions or ambiguities exist in the June 2019 Student Manual and **must be resolved by UCLM administration** before publishing the affected knowledge base entries:

1. **Excessive Class Absences â€” Grade of `DR (Dropped)` vs. Automatic Failure Grade of `5.0`:**
   - **Page 24 (Section D.7 â€” Absences from Class)** and **Page 26 (Section F.3 â€” Grades Table)** state that the maximum permitted absences are 20% of prescribed class periods (10 absences for a 3-unit lecture or 1-unit lab course), and a student who fails to withdraw and whose accumulated absences exceed 20% receives a mark of **`DR (Dropped)`**.
   - **Page 25 (Section E.3 â€” Cut System)** states that a student is allowed a limited number of absences equal to *"three (3) times the number of class meetings a week plus one. If a student incurs more cuts than allowed, he/she receives an **automatic failure grade of 5.0**."*
   - **Action Required:** The UCLM Registrar and Vice Chancellor for Academic Affairs must clarify whether instructors encode `DR` or `5.0` when a student exceeds the maximum allowable absences without officially withdrawing.

2. **Lost School ID Issuance Office â€” University Library vs. MIS / SAO:**
   - **Pages 64â€“65 (Section C and C.3.7)** state that the school ID is *"issued by the University Library"* and step 3.7 directs the student to *"Go to Library for school ID issuance."*
   - **Action Required:** Confirm whether the UCLM Library still prints and issues replacement student IDs, or if ID capture and printing at UCLM are now handled by the Information Resource and Technology Advancement Center (MIS/EDP) or SAO.

3. **Certificate of Good Moral Character (GMC) Issuing Office:**
   - **Page 14 (Section A.2.1.2)** refers to a Statement of Good Moral Character certified by the *"school dean/head and/or the student affairs director."*
   - **Page 20 (Section B.3.3)** places the issuance of a Statement of Good Moral Character under the Transfer Credential section of the **University Registrar**.
   - **Action Required:** Confirm which office at UCLM (SAO, Guidance Center, College Dean, or Registrar) is the official issuing office for a student's Certificate of Good Moral Character.

4. **Numbering Typo in Student Organization Accreditation Requirements:**
   - **Page 41 (Section J.2.1)** contains duplicate numbering (`2.1.1`, `2.1.1`, `2.1.3`, `2.1.2`, `2.1.3`) for the 8 accreditation requirements. (Cleaned into a sequential checklist in the Knowledge Base Draft).

## 4. Potentially Outdated Information (June 2019 Edition vs. Current Operations)

Because the manual is from **June 2019**, the following items are likely outdated and require verification:

1. **Digital Portal vs. Paper-Only Workflows:**
   - **Enrollment, Study Load, and Grade Slips (pp. 19, 21â€“22, 25):** The manual describes paper enrollment forms, physical study load printouts, and mailing grade reports to parents by post at the end of each term (p. 25). UCLM now utilizes digital student portal systems for enrollment, study load viewing, and grade viewing.
2. **Library Videotapes & Manual Book Cards (pp. 54â€“56):**
   - Section M.7 (p. 56) details borrowing and rewinding **videotapes** (with a PhP 20.00/day fine), and Section M.5.7 (p. 55) describes manually writing one's name, course, and year on a physical book card.
3. **Payphones and Canofile References (p. 36):**
   - Section D (p. 36) states *"Payphones for personal calls are found at the ground floor."*
4. **NSO vs. PSA Birth/Marriage Certificates (pp. 14, 63):**
   - Pages 14 and 63 refer to **NSO** Birth Certificates (now the **Philippine Statistics Authority / PSA**).
5. **Academic Calendar & Organization Renewal Deadlines (pp. 41â€“42, 88â€“89):**
   - Student organization accreditation renewal is set on or before **June 30** (expiring **July 15**), which assumed a June opening of classes. If UCLM's academic year opens in August, these deadlines may have shifted.
6. **Statutory Updates in Philippine Law:**
   - **Safe Spaces Act (RA 11313, enacted April 2019):** Page 84 cites only **RA 7877 (Anti-Sexual Harassment Act of 1995)**, which covers authority/moral ascendancy relationships (teachers, coaches, trainers). Peer-to-peer student sexual harassment and online sexual harassment are covered under RA 11313 and should be confirmed with UCLM's CODI/SAO.
   - **Anti-Hazing Act of 2018 (RA 11053):** Page 87 cites **RA 8049** (the 1995 Anti-Hazing Law), which was strengthened by RA 11053 in 2018 to prohibit all forms of hazing and expand school/organizational requirements.

## 5. Missing or Incomplete Procedures (Gaps in the Manual)

Several high-frequency student concerns in SCMS are **not covered or lack step-by-step procedures** in the June 2019 Student Manual. Authorized UCLM offices should provide approved procedures for these gaps:

1. **IT / Student Portal / LMS / Campus Wi-Fi Account Recovery:**
   - While page 10 describes the *Information Resource and Technology Advancement Center*, the manual has **no procedure** for resetting a forgotten student portal password, fixing enrollment portal errors, or reporting laboratory computer/Wi-Fi issues.
2. **Promissory Notes / Exam Permit Extensions for Unpaid Installments:**
   - Page 25 states that no student can take an official exam without a validated assessment slip from Accounting, but provides **no procedure** for how a financially distressed student applies for a Promissory Note before an exam.
3. **Completing a Mid-Term `NG` (No Grade) or Requesting a Special / Make-Up Examination:**
   - Page 27 defines `NG` as failing to take a major exam or complete requirements during the mid-term period, but does **not** specify the step-by-step procedure, deadline, or fee for taking a special examination to clear an `NG`.
4. **Filing a Grievance or Complaint Against a Teacher, Instructor, or Staff Member (Non-Sexual Harassment):**
   - Pages 77â€“80 detail the disciplinary procedure *against an erring student*, and pages 84â€“87 detail *sexual harassment complaints*, but there is **no explicit step-by-step procedure** in the manual for how a student files an academic or administrative grievance regarding an instructor's grading fairness, absenteeism, or staff conduct.
5. **Reporting Damaged Classroom / Laboratory Facilities:**
   - Page 10 identifies *Building Administration* as responsible for the physical plant and *Instructional Media Services / Building Administration* (p. 11) for AV equipment in UCLM, but no student reporting procedure is detailed.
6. **Shipboard Training / Pre-Embarkation Orientation Procedure for Maritime Cadets:**
   - Page 11 describes the *Shipboard Training Office*, and page 19 references the *Supplemental Student Manual for Maritime Education*, but specific shipboard clearance and endorsement steps are not in the general manual.
7. **Damaged or Worn-Out School ID Replacement (vs. Lost ID):**
   - Page 65 only covers the replacement of a **lost** school ID (requiring a notarized Affidavit of Loss). It does not state whether a damaged/faded ID being physically surrendered still requires a notarized Affidavit of Loss.
