### Entry G1 â€” Securing a Student Activity Permit and Reserving Campus Venues / Audio-Visual Equipment *(Co-Primary Reviewer: Building Administration & SAO/CSU; Secondary: Academic Deans & Campus Director)*

#### B.1 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `G1`
- **Topic:** Securing a Student Activity Permit and Reserving Campus Venues / Audio-Visual Equipment
- **Common student question:** "Our student organization or class wants to hold an event on campus and use a function room or sound system. How do we get an Activity Permit?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** Only duly accredited student organizations (or authorized class/college representatives) are allowed to conduct activities. An Activity Permit must be secured from the SAO at least **one (1) week before** the actual date of an on-campus activity. In the **Lapu-Lapu and Mandaue (UCLM)**, Banilad, and Mambaling campuses, the **Building Administration Office** manages the reservation of function rooms, audio-visual rooms, sound systems, and university buses. No student activity is allowed one (1) school week before Prelim, Midterm, Semi-final, or Final examinations.
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  1. **At least one (1) week before the activity:** The authorized representative of the student organization, classroom, or college secures the **Activity Permit Form** and **Waiver/Quitclaim** from the Student Affairs Office (SAO).
  2. Secure and attach the reservation form from the **Building Supervisor's / Building Administration Office** for the venue, function/AV room, sound system, or University bus reservation (if needed).
  3. Accomplish the forms completely and attach all required annexes.
  4. Obtain the written endorsement of your **Faculty Adviser** and **College Dean**.
  5. Submit the accomplished Activity Permit forms and attachments to the **SAO** and **Civil Security Unit (CSU)** for review, recommendation, and endorsement to the **Campus Director**.
  6. Wait for the **Campus Director's** approval before holding the event (if disapproved, an appeal may be made to the Vice Chancellor for Administrative Affairs).
  7. **During the activity:** The Faculty Adviser (or duly authorized representative) must be physically present for the entire duration of the activity.
  8. **Within three (3) days after the activity:** Submit an Activity Accomplishment Report and signed Financial Statement (attested by the President and Treasurer) to the SAO. Failure to submit the report within 3 days is ground for non-issuance of future activity permits.
- **Existing requirements:**
  - SAO Activity Permit Form and Waiver/Quitclaim (secured at least 1 week prior)
  - Building Administration venue / sound system / bus reservation form
  - Endorsement of the Faculty Adviser and College Dean
  - Review/endorsement by SAO and CSU, and final approval by the Campus Director
  - Compulsory presence of the Faculty Adviser during the event
  - Post-activity report and financial statement submitted to SAO within 3 days after the event
- **Responsible office according to the manual:** Student Affairs Office (SAO), Building Administration Office, Civil Security Unit (CSU), College Dean's Office, and Campus Director's Office
- **Manual page reference:** Student Manual, pages 11, 46â€“48 (PDF pages 20, 55â€“57)
