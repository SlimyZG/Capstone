#### B.1 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `F5`
- **Topic:** Exemption from Roman Catholic Retreats and Recollections
- **Common student question:** "I belong to a non-Catholic religious denomination. How can I request an exemption from attending a Catholic retreat or recollection?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** Students whose religious practices, affiliations, or denominations do not conform to or run counter to the Roman Catholic retreat or recollection activity may be exempted from attending, provided they submit a written notification to their College Dean and furnish a copy to the Campus Ministry at least one (1) week before the scheduled activity.

  1. Prepare a formal written letter requesting exemption from the retreat/recollection on account of your religious affiliation/denomination.
