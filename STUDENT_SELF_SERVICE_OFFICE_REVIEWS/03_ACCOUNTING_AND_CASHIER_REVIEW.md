### Entry E1 â€” Tuition Payment Options (Installment Schedule and 10% Cash Discount) *(Primary Reviewer: Accounting & Cashier)*

#### B.1 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `E1`
- **Topic:** Tuition Payment Options (Installment Schedule and 10% Cash Discount)
- **Common student question:** "How is tuition divided if I pay in installments, and is there a discount if I pay in full cash?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** All fees are computed on a semester/term basis and may be paid in cash or in four (4) equal installments corresponding to the four major examination periods. Students who pay their tuition in full cash on or before the posted deadline receive a **10% discount on tuition** (excluding non-academic subjects).
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  - **For Full Cash Payment (10% Tuition Discount):**
    1. Check the Accounting Department bulletin board for the full-payment discount deadline.
    2. Pay your full semester assessment at the Cashier on or before the deadline to receive the 10% tuition discount (excluding non-academic subjects).
  - **For Installment Payment:**
    1. Pay the entrance/down payment upon enrollment.
    2. Pay the remaining school fees in four (4) equal installments before each major examination period:
       - **Prelim:** 25% of total assessment plus old account, less down payment
       - **Midterm:** 25% of total assessment plus old account, less down payment
       - **Semi-final:** 25% of total assessment plus old account, less down payment
       - **Final:** 25% of total assessment plus old account, less down payment
- **Existing requirements:**
  - Student assessment form / statement of account
- **Responsible office according to the manual:** Accounting Department / Cashier
- **Manual page reference:** Student Manual, page 31 (PDF page 40)
