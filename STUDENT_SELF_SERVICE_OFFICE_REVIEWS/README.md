## 1. Purpose

This directory contains **office-specific validation and sign-off packets** prepared for authorized **University of Cebu Lapu-Lapu and Mandaue (UCLM)** personnel.

Before the **Student Self-Service Assistance** feature (`SUGGEST / Guidance Only`) is implemented in SCMS, every procedure, requirement, fee, schedule, and responsible office extracted from the **June 2019 Student Manual** must be reviewed, verified, corrected (if outdated), and formally signed off by the responsible UCLM office.

### Key Feature Principles (`SUGGEST / Guidance Only`)
1. **Guidance Only:** When a student describes a question or concern, SCMS displays verified Student Manual guidance, requirements, responsible office(s), and page references.
2. **Strictly Optional:** Students are never forced to follow self-service steps before submitting a formal concern.
3. **No Automatic Ticket Creation or Resolution:** Viewing guidance never automatically creates a concern and never automatically resolves or closes an existing concern.
4. **Unchanged Core SCMS Workflow:** If the student still needs assistance, they can proceed directly to the existing formal SCMS concern submission, classification, routing, and oversight workflow.
5. **Sensitive & Urgent Safeguard:** Sensitive, safety, harassment, violence, hazing, disciplinary, and emergency entries (`S1â€“S7`) are **formal reporting and emergency assistance pathways**, never ordinary "do-it-yourself" self-help instructions.

## 4. Review Instructions for UCLM Personnel

Authorized UCLM reviewers are requested to follow these six (6) steps when completing their assigned review packet:

1. **Read the Assigned Entries:** Review the extracted `Common Student Question`, `Current Manual-Based Answer`, `Existing Step-by-Step Procedure`, `Existing Requirements`, `Responsible Office According to the Manual`, and `Manual Page Reference` in Section B of your packet.
2. **Verify Procedures Against Current UCLM Practices:** Answer the targeted `Office Confirmation Questions` in Section C of each entry and resolve any highlighted `Special Issues` assigned to your office.
3. **Complete the Reviewer Corrections Fields:** In Section D of each entry, record any updated 2026 procedures, documentary requirements, current fees, processing timelines/schedules, or corrected responsible office names.
4. **Provide Supporting Official References:** Attach or cite any updated UCLM memorandum, CHED Memorandum Order (CMO), student form, fee schedule, or supplemental college manual that supersedes or supplements the June 2019 Student Manual.
5. **Select an Approval Decision:** In Section E (`Approval Record`), mark whether the entry is `Pending`, `Approved`, or `Requires Revision`.
6. **Sign the Approval Record:** Print name, official position, office, review date, and authorized signature in Section E.

---

## 5. Institutional Approval Rules

- **No Automatic Approval:** No knowledge base entry is approved automatically. Every entry currently remains in **`Pending UCLM Validation`** status.
- **Authorized Sign-Off Only:** Only authorized UCLM personnel (Office Heads, Directors, Deans, or designated officers) may approve content for their functional domain.
- **Controlled Knowledge Base Updates:** Reviewer corrections must be reflected in the knowledge base only after formal review and sign-off are completed.
- **Strict Publication Gate:** Unverified or unapproved entries must **never** be published or displayed to students in SCMS.
