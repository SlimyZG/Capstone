### Entry B1 â€” Borrowing Library Books and Materials *(Primary Reviewer: UCLM Library)*

#### B.1 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `B1`
- **Topic:** Borrowing Library Books and Materials
- **Common student question:** "How do I borrow books from the library, how many books can I borrow, and how long can I keep them?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** All *bona fide* students can borrow library materials by presenting their validated school ID at the loan desk and legibly filling out the book card. Borrowing limits and loan durations depend on the type of book: Reference books (up to 2 books for 3 days, renewable for 3 days), Reserved books (1 hour inside the library, or overnight from 5:00 PM to 8:00 AM the next day), and Fiction books (1 week, renewable if not in demand). General references, Filipiniana books, periodicals, theses, and CDs/DVDs are for room/library reading only. One week before final examinations, books can no longer be borrowed for home use.
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  1. Wear your valid school ID upon entering the library ("No school ID, no entry").
  2. Locate the material and proceed to the library loan desk.
  3. Present your validated school ID to the loan desk staff.
  4. Fill out the book card legibly with your full name, course, and year level.
  5. When leaving the library, open and show your bag, folders, and large envelopes for security inspection, and present the due date card of every library material being taken outside.
- **Existing requirements:**
  - Validated school ID (non-transferable; using another student's ID results in confiscation)
  - Legibly signed book card with name, course, and year
- **Responsible office according to the manual:** Library and Electronic Resource Services
- **Manual page reference:** Student Manual, pages 53â€“55 (PDF pages 62â€“64)

### Entry B2 â€” Overdue Library Books â€” Fines and Penalties *(Primary Reviewer: UCLM Library; Secondary: Cashier)*

#### B.2 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `B2`
- **Topic:** Overdue Library Books â€” Fines and Penalties
- **Common student question:** "How much is the fine if I return a borrowed library book late?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** The Library imposes overdue fines depending on the category of the borrowed material, and computation includes Sundays and Holidays. Habitual delinquency (frequently returning books late or disregarding library rules) results in the suspension of borrowing privileges. Note that the manual states fines may vary per campus.
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  1. Return the overdue book to the library loan desk as soon as possible.
  2. Have the loan desk officer compute the accrued overdue fine based on the book type:
     - **Reserved books:** PhP 1.00 for the first hour and PhP 0.50 for every succeeding hour (including Sundays and Holidays).
     - **Reference books:** PhP 1.00 per day (including Sundays and Holidays).
     - **Fiction books:** PhP 0.50 per day (including Sundays and Holidays).
     - **Unreturned reserved books and periodicals borrowed for photocopying:** PhP 10.00 per day.
     - **Videotapes:** PhP 20.00 per day.
  3. Settle the fine according to the Library and Cashier payment procedure.
- **Existing requirements:**
  - Return of the overdue library material
  - Payment of computed overdue fines
- **Responsible office according to the manual:** Library and Electronic Resource Services and Cashier / Accounting Department

### Entry B3 â€” Reporting and Replacing a Lost Library Book *(Primary Reviewer: UCLM Library; Secondary: Cashier)*

#### B.3 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `B3`
- **Topic:** Reporting and Replacing a Lost Library Book
- **Common student question:** "I lost a book I borrowed from the library. What should I do and how much will I be charged?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** Lost library books must be reported immediately to the loan desk officer so that daily overdue fines stop accruing. The student must replace or pay for the lost book within fifteen (15) days, after which unsettled charges are reported to the Cashier's Office and billed to the student's account.
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  1. Report the lost book immediately to the library loan desk officer.
  2. Within fifteen (15) days of reporting, either replace the book or settle the payment covering:
     - Current price of the book;
     - Processing fee of PhP 25.00; and
     - Accrued overdue fines up to the date of settlement/reporting.
  3. If not settled within 15 days, the Library forwards the charges to the Cashier's Office to be charged to your student account (which must be cleared before graduation or transfer clearance).
- **Existing requirements:**
  - Immediate report to the library loan desk officer
  - Replacement copy of the book (or payment of the current book price) + PhP 25.00 processing fee + accrued fines within 15 days
- **Responsible office according to the manual:** Library and Electronic Resource Services and Cashier / Accounting Department
- **Manual page reference:** Student Manual, page 55 (PDF page 64)
- **Current validation status:** `Pending UCLM Validation`
