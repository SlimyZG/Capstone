### Entry A2 â€” Non-Wearing of School ID and Prohibited ID Acts *(Primary Reviewer: SAO & CSU; Secondary: College Deans)*

#### B.2 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `A2`
- **Topic:** Non-Wearing of School ID and Prohibited ID Acts
- **Common student question:** "What happens if I forget to bring or wear my school ID on campus, or if I borrow a classmate's ID?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** Every student must wear their valid school ID at all times inside the campus ("No school ID, no entry" also applies to the library). Non-wearing of the school ID is subject to progressive disciplinary sanctions from the 1st to the 4th offense. Furthermore, using another student's school ID or study load, lending one's school ID, changing the picture on the ID, or tampering with the ID to make it appear as one's own are strictly prohibited and classified as serious offenses involving dishonesty.
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  1. If cited for a **1st offense** of non-wearing of school ID: An authorized University official issues a verbal warning and logs/reports the infraction to the College Head/Dean or SAO.
  2. If cited for a **2nd offense**: The student must submit a written explanation bearing their parent's or guardian's signature, after which the Head/Dean or SAO issues a written reprimand.
  3. If cited for a **3rd offense**: The Head/Dean, SAO, or authorized official sends the repeated offender home for the day, which is recorded as an official absence.
  4. If cited for a **4th offense**: The Head/Dean, SAO, or authorized official recommends the student for investigation for approval of a one-week suspension from class (recorded as official absences).
- **Existing requirements:**
  - Validated school ID worn visibly at all times inside the campus
  - For 2nd offense of non-wearing: Written explanation signed by the student's parent or guardian
- **Responsible office according to the manual:** Civil Security Unit (CSU), Student Affairs Office (SAO), and College Dean/Chairperson
- **Manual page reference:** Student Manual, pages 64â€“65, 68 (PDF pages 73â€“74, 78)

#### B.3 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `A3`
- **Topic:** Uniform Exemption Application
- **Common student question:** "I am a working student / pregnant / cross-enrollee / married. How do I apply for a school uniform exemption?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** Students who cannot wear the prescribed school uniform due to a valid reason qualified under University policy must apply for a Uniform Exemption Card from the Student Affairs Office (SAO) at the start of classes. The Uniform Exemption Card is non-transferable, must be presented to the campus security guard upon entry, and must be renewed every semester.
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  1. Ask for a Uniform Exemption Application Form from the Student Affairs Office (SAO) at the start of classes.
  2. Fill out the application form and attach the required supporting documents along with two (2) 2x2 colored pictures.
  3. Submit the accomplished application form and attachments to the SAO for evaluation and approval.
  4. Upon approval by the SAO, pay the required fee for the Exemption Card at the Cashier.
  5. Submit the official receipt to the SAO as proof of payment.
  6. Claim your Uniform Exemption Card from the SAO and present it to the security guard upon entering the University.
- **Existing requirements:**
  - Qualifications (student must fall under one of the following categories):
    - Pregnant women
    - Married students
    - Students legitimately working with entities, firms, or establishments not owned by the applicant's family or relatives
    - Students over 35 years of age
    - Cross-enrollees
    - Second coursers
    - Special cases
  - Documentary requirements:
    - Accomplished Uniform Exemption Application Form (available at SAO)
    - Two (2) 2x2 colored pictures
    - Authenticated marriage certificate (for married women)
    - Authenticated photocopy of Permit to Cross-Enroll (for cross-enrollees)
    - NSO (now PSA) Birth Certificate (for age verification)
    - Certificate of Employment (for working student / employee applicants)

### Entry G1 â€” Securing a Student Activity Permit and Reserving Campus Venues / Audio-Visual Equipment *(Primary Reviewer: SAO & CSU, Building Administration; Secondary: College Deans & Campus Director)*

#### B.6 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `G1`
- **Topic:** Securing a Student Activity Permit and Reserving Campus Venues / Audio-Visual Equipment
- **Common student question:** "Our student organization or class wants to hold an event on campus and use a function room or sound system. How do we get an Activity Permit?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** Only duly accredited student organizations (or authorized class/college representatives) are allowed to conduct activities. An Activity Permit must be secured from the SAO at least **one (1) week before** the actual date of an on-campus activity. In the **Lapu-Lapu and Mandaue (UCLM)**, Banilad, and Mambaling campuses, the **Building Administration Office** manages the reservation of function rooms, audio-visual rooms, sound systems, and university buses. No student activity is allowed one (1) school week before Prelim, Midterm, Semi-final, or Final examinations.
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  1. **At least one (1) week before the activity:** The authorized representative of the student organization, classroom, or college secures the **Activity Permit Form** and **Waiver/Quitclaim** from the Student Affairs Office (SAO).
  2. Secure and attach the reservation form from the **Building Supervisor's / Building Administration Office** for the venue, function/AV room, sound system, or University bus reservation (if needed).
  3. Accomplish the forms completely and attach all required annexes.
  4. Obtain the written endorsement of your **Faculty Adviser** and **College Dean**.
  5. Submit the accomplished Activity Permit forms and attachments to the **SAO** and **Civil Security Unit (CSU)** for review, recommendation, and endorsement to the **Campus Director**.
  6. Wait for the **Campus Director's** approval before holding the event (if disapproved, an appeal may be made to the Vice Chancellor for Administrative Affairs).
  7. **During the activity:** The Faculty Adviser (or duly authorized representative) must be physically present for the entire duration of the activity.
  8. **Within three (3) days after the activity:** Submit an Activity Accomplishment Report and signed Financial Statement (attested by the President and Treasurer) to the SAO. Failure to submit the report within 3 days is ground for non-issuance of future activity permits.
- **Existing requirements:**
  - SAO Activity Permit Form and Waiver/Quitclaim (secured at least 1 week prior)
  - Building Administration venue / sound system / bus reservation form
  - Endorsement of the Faculty Adviser and College Dean
  - Review/endorsement by SAO and CSU, and final approval by the Campus Director
  - Compulsory presence of the Faculty Adviser during the event
  - Post-activity report and financial statement submitted to SAO within 3 days after the event
- **Responsible office according to the manual:** Student Affairs Office (SAO), Building Administration Office, Civil Security Unit (CSU), College Dean's Office, and Campus Director's Office
- **Manual page reference:** Student Manual, pages 11, 46â€“48 (PDF pages 20, 55â€“57)

### Entry G4 â€” Off-Campus Activities (Educational Tours / Field Trips) and Rights of Non-Participating Students *(Co-Primary Reviewer: SAO & Academic Deans; Secondary: Medical Clinic)*

#### B.9 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `G4`
- **Topic:** Off-Campus Activities (Educational Tours / Field Trips) and Rights of Non-Participating Students
- **Common student question:** "Am I required to join an off-campus field trip or educational tour, and what happens to my grade if I cannot join?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** Consistent with CHED Memorandum Order No. 63, series of 2017, **participation in any off-campus activity is strictly voluntary and not compulsory**. An off-campus activity cannot be made a substitute for a major examination to compel students to join. Furthermore, the proponent/instructor **must provide a complementary or substitute activity** for students who cannot participate or opt not to join. A non-participating student **must not be given a failing mark** for not joining the off-campus trip and shall instead be graded based on their accomplishment of the substitute activity.
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  - **If you are opting NOT to join an off-campus activity:**
    1. Inform your subject instructor/proponent that you cannot participate in the off-campus activity.
    2. Request and accomplish the required complementary/substitute academic activity so you can be graded fairly without penalty.
  - **If you ARE participating in an approved off-campus activity:**
    1. Attend the mandatory pre-activity consultation/orientation and pre-departure briefing (covering destination, itemized costs with no hidden charges, and emergency preparedness).
    2. Secure and submit a **Medical Clearance** issued by the University Medical Clinic.
    3. Submit a **duly notarized Parent's/Guardian's Permit/Consent** (failure to submit this notarized consent disallows participation).
    4. Ensure individual or group insurance is arranged and complete the post-activity debriefing and learning/observation journal after returning.
- **Existing requirements:**
  - For participating students: University Clinic Medical Clearance, duly notarized Parent's/Guardian's Permit/Consent, individual/group insurance, and observation/learning journal
  - For proponents/organizers: SAO Activity Permit submitted at least 1 month prior, notarized Certificate/Report of Compliance submitted at least 15 days prior, 1:35â€“50 teacher-student ratio, accredited tour guides, and valid vehicle registration/insurance
- **Responsible office according to the manual:** Subject Proponent / College Dean's Office, Student Affairs Office (SAO), Medical Clinic, and Campus Director / Vice Chancellor for Academics/Administration
