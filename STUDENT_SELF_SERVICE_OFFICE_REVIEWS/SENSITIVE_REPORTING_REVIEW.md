### Entry S7 â€” Confidential Random Drug Testing Protocol (`Guidelines on the Conduct of Random Drug Testing`)

#### 1. Entry ID & Topic
- **Entry ID:** `S7`
- **Topic:** Confidential Random Drug Testing & Positive Result Intervention Protocol
- **Current validation status:** `Pending UCLM Validation`

#### 2. Existing Manual-Based Reporting Information
- **Nature of Concern (pp. 97â€“105):** Questions or concerns regarding student rights, strict statutory confidentiality, and non-incrimination during random drug testing under Dangerous Drugs Board (DDB) Regulation No. 6, s. 2003 (as amended by DDB Regulation No. 3, s. 2009 / Republic Act No. 9165).
- **Official Policy & Channel Stated in the Manual (pp. 97â€“105):**
  - **Strict Confidentiality (pp. 98, 101â€“102):** All drug test results and records are treated with **utmost secrecy and confidentiality**. Under Section 72 of RA 9165, any person who violates the confidentiality of records or divulges drug test results to anyone other than the student concerned and their parents (or the Drug Testing Coordinator / Drug Counselor / attending physician) faces criminal and administrative penalties.
  - **Non-Disciplinary / Non-Incrimination Guarantee (pp. 98, 102â€“103):** A positive confirmatory drug test result under random drug testing **shall not be a ground for expulsion or any disciplinary action** against the student, shall not be reflected in any academic record, and shall not be used to incriminate the student in any administrative, civil, or criminal proceeding.
  - **Confidential Health Intervention Pathway (pp. 101â€“103):** Confirmed positive cases are handled confidentially through a private conference with the **Drug Testing Coordinator**, **Drug Counselor (Guidance Counselor)**, the student, the parent, and a DOH-accredited physician to determine the appropriate counseling or treatment intervention level.
