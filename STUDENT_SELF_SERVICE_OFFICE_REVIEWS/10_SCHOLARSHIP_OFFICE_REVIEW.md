#### B.1 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `E3`
- **Topic:** Applying for Scholarships and Financial Aid (Academic, Dean's List, Working Scholar, Athletics, Cultural, Service Units)
- **Common student question:** "What scholarships are available at UC and what are the grade requirements to apply or maintain my scholarship?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** The Scholarship Office disseminates scholarship information, accepts applications, and monitors scholars. Available grants include:
  1. **Academic & Dean's List Scholarships:** For high school honor graduates, National Science High School graduates, and college Dean's Listers. Required grades to avail/maintain:
     - *Summa Cum Laude tier:* 1.00â€“1.20 GPA with no grade below 2.00
     - *Magna Cum Laude tier:* 1.21â€“1.45 GPA with no grade below 2.30
     - *Cum Laude tier:* 1.46â€“1.70 GPA with no grade below 2.50
  2. **Working Scholarship:** Open to high school graduates with a general average of **90% and above**, or college students with a **2.5 GPA and above**. Selection also considers the IQ exam and personality test administered by the Guidance Office, as well as family income.
  3. **Athletics Scholarship:** For official varsity team members (10% to 100% tuition discount based on achievement).
  4. **Cultural Scholarship:** For members of official UC cultural organizations in good standing (25% to 100% tuition discount via Student Cultural Services).
  5. **Service Unit Scholarships:** Tuition discounts for active members of the Campus Ministry Choir, SAO Volunteers, Student Organization Leaders, Guidance Peer Facilitators, Student Publication Members, and NSTP, subject to Scholarship Office policies.
  6. **Maritime & Corporate Scholarships:** International and domestic maritime sponsors (e.g., NSA, IMEC, INC) and institutional grants (Don Sulpicio, Gothong Lines, Gotianuy, Menzi/MPRT).
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  1. Visit the **Scholarship Office** (or the respective sponsoring unit: Athletics Department, Student Cultural Services, or Maritime Scholarship Office) to inquire about open application slots and specific application forms.
  2. For **Working Scholarship** applicants: Present your report card (90%+ HS average) or college grades (2.5+ GPA) and family income documents, and take the required IQ and personality tests at the **Guidance Office**.
  3. For **Dean's List** discount applicants: Ensure your semester GPA meets the 1.00â€“1.70 honor tier with no grade below the cutoff, and coordinate with your College Dean and the Scholarship Office.
- **Existing requirements:**
  - Grade credentials meeting the specific scholarship cutoff
  - For Working Scholars: 90%+ HS grade or 2.5+ college GPA, Guidance Office IQ & personality test results, and proof of family income
