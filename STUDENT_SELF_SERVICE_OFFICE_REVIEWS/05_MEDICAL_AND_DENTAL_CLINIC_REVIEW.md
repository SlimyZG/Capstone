### Entry F1 â€” Availing University Medical Clinic Services and Free Initial Medicines *(Primary Reviewer: UCLM Medical Clinic)*

#### B.1 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `F1`
- **Topic:** Availing University Medical Clinic Services and Free Initial Medicines
- **Common student question:** "How do I visit the school clinic for a check-up or medicine, and what do I need to bring?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** Students with medical problems can avail themselves of the University clinic's services on a first-come, first-served basis during clinic hours (**8:00 AM to 9:00 PM on school days, no noon break**; hours may vary per campus). Free initial doses of medicine are given for minor ailments (headache, abdominal pain, diarrhea, cough, colds, and fever), but medicines are dispensed **only to the student concerned in person** (never to a representative) to avoid untoward side effects.
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  1. Proceed personally to the University Medical Clinic during clinic hours.
  2. Present any one of the following proofs of official enrollment:
     - Valid school ID;
     - Official study load;
     - Library card; or
     - Certification from any University personnel or teacher certifying that you are officially enrolled.
  3. Undergo consultation, vital signs check, or treatment (blood pressure taking, wound dressing, nebulization, physical exam, or initial medicine dosage) with the clinic nurse or University physician.
- **Existing requirements:**
  - Personal appearance of the student (medicines are never dispensed to a representative)
  - Valid school ID, study load, library card, or enrollment certification from a teacher/personnel
- **Responsible office according to the manual:** Medical and Dental Services (University Medical Clinic)

### Entry F2 â€” Validation of Outside Medical Certificates and Medical Clearance for Absences or PE Exemption *(Primary Reviewer: UCLM Medical Clinic / University Physician)*

#### B.2 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `F2`
- **Topic:** Validation of Outside Medical Certificates and Medical Clearance for Absences or PE Exemption
- **Common student question:** "I was absent due to illness and have a medical certificate from an outside doctor. Will my instructor accept it directly?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** **No.** Medical certificates issued by an outside physician **must be validated by the school physician within two (2) weeks from issuance**. Only medical certificates validated by the University physician will be accepted by instructors. Additionally, certifications for Physical Education (PE) exemption are granted only by the University physician after a thorough physical examination is completed at the University clinic.
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  - **To validate an outside medical certificate for class absences:**
    1. Bring the original medical certificate issued by your attending outside physician to the University Medical Clinic **within two (2) weeks from its date of issuance**.
    2. Present your valid school ID or study load and have the University physician examine/validate the certificate.
    3. Present the validated medical certificate to your subject instructor(s).
  - **To request a Medical Clearance (for absences, off-campus activities, or SOLAS) or PE Exemption:**
    1. Undergo a thorough physical examination by the University clinic's physician.
    2. Receive the official Medical Certificate / Clearance / PE Exemption Certification issued by the University physician.
- **Existing requirements:**
  - Valid school ID or study load
  - Original medical certificate from the outside physician (must be submitted for validation within 2 weeks of issuance)
- **Responsible office according to the manual:** Medical and Dental Services (University Physician)
- **Manual page reference:** Student Manual, pages 57â€“58 (PDF pages 66â€“67)

### Entry F3 â€” Availing University Dental Clinic Services and Tooth Extraction *(Primary Reviewer: UCLM Dental Clinic)*

#### B.3 Extracted Entry Details
- **Entry ID:** `F3`
- **Topic:** Availing University Dental Clinic Services and Tooth Extraction
- **Common student question:** "What dental services are free at the school dental clinic, and what are the requirements for tooth extraction?"
- **Current manual-based answer:** The University Dental Clinic is open from **8:00 AMâ€“12:00 NN and 1:00 PMâ€“5:00 PM during school days** (hours may vary per campus) on a first-come, first-served basis. It provides daily consultations, annual dental exams (for 1st-year students, transferees, returnees, and PE 1 students), temporary tooth fillings, oral prophylaxis, simple tooth extractions, initial doses of prescribed post-extraction medicines, and dental certificates (issued only to students who underwent extraction or have consultation records at the clinic). Complicated cases (impacted/third molars, prosthodontics, orthodontics, endodontics, periodontal problems) are referred to private dentists or hospitals.
- **Existing step-by-step procedure:**
  1. Visit the University Dental Clinic during operating hours and present your valid school ID, study load, library card, or teacher certification of enrollment.
  2. If you are seeking a **simple tooth extraction**:
     - **If you are a minor (below 18 years old):** You must be accompanied by your parent or guardian with a duly signed dental clearance.
     - **If you have a medical condition (e.g., heart illness, asthma, diabetes):** You must first submit a medical certificate/clearance from your attending physician.
  3. After treatment or extraction, receive your initial medicine dosage and, if applicable, a dental certificate or temporary exemption from classes/duties.
- **Existing requirements:**
  - Valid school ID, study load, library card, or enrollment certification
  - For minor students seeking tooth extraction: Personal accompaniment by a parent/guardian and a duly signed dental clearance
  - For patients with medical compromises (heart illness, asthma, diabetes, etc.): Medical certificate from an attending physician
- **Responsible office according to the manual:** Medical and Dental Services (University Dental Clinic)
